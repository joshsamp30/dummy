When Do Air Ducts Need Professional Attention?

Excessive dust, uneven room temperatures, rising utility bills — the warning signs that your ductwork needs help, and how to fix it.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Ductwork Service

A home's ductwork is a critical part of the HVAC system, but it can also cause hidden problems that necessitate professional ductwork services. High energy bills or rooms that fail to reach comfortable temperatures are often the result of damaged or dirty air ducts. Understanding these key indicators can help homeowners address issues promptly and restore their home's heating and cooling performance.

Ductwork inspection

Signs Your Ductwork Needs Repair

A home's air ducts operate quietly behind the walls, but problems become apparent when something goes wrong. Early recognition of ductwork issues can save homeowners hundreds of dollars in energy costs and prevent the HVAC system from overworking. Many homeowners overlook these warning signs until comfort and efficiency are significantly affected.

Excessive dust buildup is often the first indicator that ductwork needs attention. If dust accumulates on surfaces quickly or particles are visible in sunlight, the duct system may have leaks or disconnected sections. These gaps allow contaminated air from attics, crawl spaces and wall cavities to enter the system and circulate throughout living spaces.

Temperature inconsistencies between rooms can also signal serious duct problems. For example, one room may feel excessively warm while another remains cool. Damaged or poorly sealed ducts allow conditioned air to escape before it reaches certain areas. According to ENERGY STAR, leaky ducts can reduce HVAC efficiency by up to 20%, causing the system to run longer cycles.

Other signs include unexplained increases in utility bills during peak heating or cooling periods, as well as unusual noises such as whistling, rattling or banging from vents. These sounds often indicate loose connections, damaged sections or restricted airflow that require professional duct repair services.

Common Ductwork Issues We Find

  • Disconnected duct boots — where the supply duct meets the register, the connection has come loose or never been properly sealed. We see this constantly in homes that have been remodelled.
  • Mastic-failed seams on metal ducts where joints have shifted or original sealant has hardened and cracked.
  • Crushed flex duct — flexible ductwork pinched, kinked or compressed, often by storage in attic or crawl space.
  • Undersized ductwork — original ducts inadequate for the cooling load of modern AC systems, causing static-pressure problems.
  • Missing or compressed insulation on ducts running through unconditioned space, causing thermal losses on every cycle.
  • Disconnected dryer/bath exhaust dumping moisture and lint into attic instead of outside.

Duct Cleaning vs. Duct Repair

These are different services. Duct cleaning removes accumulated dust, pet dander and debris from the inside of your ducts using truck-mounted negative-pressure equipment with mechanical agitation. Duct cleaning is appropriate every 5–10 years, more often if you have pets, severe allergies or recent construction work. Duct repair fixes leaks, replaces damaged sections and seals connections. Repair is appropriate when there's airflow, comfort or efficiency problems traceable to the ducts themselves.

How We Diagnose Duct Problems

Our duct diagnosis process uses calibrated tools, not guesswork. We perform a duct leakage test that pressurises the duct system with a calibrated fan and measures total leakage in CFM at 25 pascals. We also use thermal imaging to find leaks and missing insulation, smoke pencils to trace airflow at register seams, and pressure-mapping tools to identify zones with inadequate or excessive airflow. The result is a written report identifying exactly what's wrong and what to fix in priority order.
