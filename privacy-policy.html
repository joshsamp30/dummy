Privacy Policy

Eric Kjelshus Energy Heating and Cooling understands your concerns about privacy and security when using the Internet, and we are committed to protecting the privacy of our valued customers. We do not sell, rent or exchange any information collected via the Internet. We keep all collected information confidential, and we have implemented security policies and technical measures to protect your personal data from improper use or unauthorized access.

What We Collect

To fulfill your requests for our services and products, we obtain your name, address, phone number, email and a description of the work you need. We use this information to schedule services, deliver products, respond to your requests, and (with your permission) to contact you about products, services, events, special offers or promotions.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to deliver the services you've asked us to perform. By using our website you consent to the collection and use of data for this purpose. We do not sell, trade or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties without prior notification.

Text Messages

Your consent to receive text messages from us is treated with the utmost confidentiality. We use SMS for marketing, notifications and other communications under strict compliance with applicable laws. Your opt-in for these messages is never shared with any third parties. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember preferences and analyse traffic. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies; refusing them will not prevent you from using the site.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to safeguard the data you submit. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, but we work to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information.

Updates

Any changes to this policy will be posted on the website. Please check this page periodically.
