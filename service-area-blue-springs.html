Blue Springs is one of our busiest service areas — and it's been that way for decades. The mix of mature subdivisions like Chapman Farms and Stone Canyon, lakefront homes near Adams Park and Blue Springs Lake, and a steady inflow of new construction means our technicians see the full range of HVAC and plumbing situations every week. Eric Kjelshus has personally consulted on countless Blue Springs installations since the early 1970s, and our local reputation is built on showing up, doing the work right and standing behind it.

Cooling Services in Blue Springs

Air conditioning is non-negotiable in a Blue Springs summer. We provide AC repair, maintenance, central AC installation, ductless mini-split installation and full system replacement. Common service calls in Blue Springs include refrigerant leaks on aging systems, capacitor failures during heat waves, frozen evaporator coils caused by dirty filters, and oversized AC equipment that short-cycles and fails to dehumidify the home properly. Our NATE-certified technicians diagnose the root cause and quote the repair before any work starts. View AC services.

Heating Services in Blue Springs

Furnaces, boilers, heat pumps and radiant — repaired or replaced same-day. Blue Springs has a wide range of heating equipment ages, and we treat each one according to its specific situation. A 25-year-old gas furnace that's been routinely maintained is often a candidate for replacement (because the heat exchanger is at end-of-life and efficiency has dropped); a five-year-old high-efficiency unit usually just needs a tune-up and filter change. We won't push you into replacement when repair is the right call. View heating services.

Plumbing in Blue Springs

Water heaters, tankless installations, burst pipe repair, sump pumps and gas leak detection. Blue Springs' clay-heavy soil makes basement water management especially important — we install primary sump pumps, battery-backup systems and Wi-Fi-connected pumps that text you the moment a problem is detected. Lakefront homes often have higher hard-water mineral content; we install whole-home softeners that protect water heaters and fixtures from scaling. View plumbing services.

Electrical in Blue Springs

LED retrofits, generators, EV chargers, surge protection and panel upgrades by Missouri master electricians. Blue Springs gets more than its share of summer storms — we install whole-home surge protection at the panel and standby generators that automatically run essential or whole-home loads during outages. View electrical services.

Geothermal in Blue Springs

Many Blue Springs lots are large enough to accommodate horizontal geothermal loops, and the local soil has good thermal conductivity. We've installed dozens of geothermal systems across Blue Springs and have customers cutting heating and cooling bills by 60–90% versus conventional equipment. With the 30% federal tax credit through 2032, the financial case has never been stronger. View geothermal services.

Indoor Air Quality & Energy Audits

Whole-home filtration, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, UV sanitizers and smart thermostats. Our BPI-certified energy audits use blower door testing, infrared imaging, combustion analysis and duct leakage testing to identify exactly where your home is wasting energy — and what to fix first. The audit qualifies your home for federal 25C tax credits up to $1,200/year on insulation and air-sealing improvements.
