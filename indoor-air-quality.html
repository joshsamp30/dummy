Indoor Air Quality in Kansas City

Whole-home filtration, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, UV purification, ductwork services and smart thermostats — the full toolkit for cleaner, healthier air at home.

Indoor air is often two to five times more polluted than outdoor air, according to the EPA. The U.S. average household spends roughly 90% of its time indoors, which means the air inside your home or office has more impact on your respiratory health, allergy load, sleep quality and cognitive performance than the air outside ever will. Eric Kjelshus Energy has performed more than 15,000 indoor air quality tests across the Kansas City metro and we know exactly what to test for, what each result means, and how to fix the problems we find.

Symptoms of poor indoor air quality include headaches, fatigue, sinus congestion, throat irritation, allergy flare-ups, asthma attacks, dry skin, and sleep disruption. Long-term exposure to high levels of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), formaldehyde, particulate matter or carbon monoxide is linked to heart disease, lung disease and certain cancers. The good news: nearly every IAQ problem we find is fixable, often with equipment that pays for itself in HVAC efficiency and reduced sick days within a year or two.

Whole-Home Air Filtration & Purification

The 1-inch fiberglass filter that came with your furnace is only there to protect the equipment, not your lungs. Real indoor air filtration requires a media filter (4–5 inches deep) or higher. We install three primary categories of whole-home air cleaners:

  • Media air cleaners trap large particles and extend the life of your HVAC system. Excellent first line of defence and the most affordable option.
  • Electronic air cleaners use electrostatic attraction to capture much smaller particles than a media filter alone.
  • HEPA air cleaners capture 99.97% of airborne particles down to 0.3 microns — the gold standard for households with severe allergies, asthma or compromised immunity.

UV Air Sanitizers

Ultraviolet (UV-C) light at the right wavelength destroys the DNA of bacteria, viruses and mold spores as they pass over the lamp. Installed in the supply plenum or near the evaporator coil, a UV air sanitizer continuously kills microbes that would otherwise circulate through your home. UV systems pair beautifully with a high-MERV filter — the filter catches what the UV light has already killed.

Humidifiers & Dehumidifiers

Indoor humidity should sit between 30% and 50% year-round. Below 30%, occupants experience dry skin, irritated sinuses, static shocks, increased dust accumulation and cracked wood furniture. Above 60%, the home becomes a breeding ground for mold, dust mites and bacteria; HVAC systems work harder and clammy summer rooms feel uncomfortable even at otherwise reasonable temperatures.

We install whole-home humidifiers for the dry winter months — Aprilaire, Honeywell and Carrier units that integrate with your existing furnace or air handler. We install whole-home dehumidifiers for the muggy spring and summer months — both standalone Aprilaire/Santa Fe units and integrated solutions that work with the cooling system. Either way, you get steady, automatic humidity control without thinking about it.

Ductwork Cleaning, Repair & Design

Leaky ductwork can cut HVAC efficiency by up to 20% (per ENERGY STAR) and pulls dust, allergens and rodent contamination from attics, crawl spaces and wall cavities into your living space. We perform duct leakage testing with calibrated fans, seal leaks with mastic or aerosol sealant, repair physically damaged sections, and (where needed) redesign ductwork that was sized incorrectly. Our ductwork design services use software to model airflow and ensure every register delivers the right CFM.

Routine duct cleaning is appropriate every 5–10 years for most homes — more often if you have pets, allergies or recent remodeling work. We use truck-mounted negative-pressure equipment with mechanical agitation that actually removes the debris rather than just stirring it up.

Smart Thermostats & Zoning

A smart thermostat saves 10–23% on heating and cooling annually (per ENERGY STAR), learns your schedule, lets you control the system from your phone, and integrates with Alexa, Google Home and Apple HomeKit. We install Ecobee, Nest, Honeywell and Carrier smart thermostats and zoning systems that let different parts of your home heat or cool independently.

Heat & Energy Recovery Ventilators (HRV/ERV)

Modern homes are built tight. That's great for energy efficiency but bad for indoor air quality — the same insulation and weatherstripping that keeps conditioned air in also keeps stale air, CO2, VOCs and humidity in. An HRV or ERV exchanges stale indoor air for fresh outdoor air while recovering 70–90% of the heat (or coolth, in summer) from the outgoing air. The result is fresher air with virtually no energy penalty.

Indoor Air Quality FAQ

How do I know if my air quality is bad?

Symptoms include unexplained headaches, fatigue, allergy flare-ups, dust accumulation, musty smells and frequent illness. The only definitive answer is a professional test. We test for particulate matter, VOCs, CO2, humidity, formaldehyde, carbon monoxide and radon.

How often should I change my filter?

1-inch filters: every 30 days. Pleated filters: every 60–90 days. 4–5-inch media filters: every 6–12 months. HEPA filters: 12 months or per manufacturer.

Can a UV light replace my filter?

No. UV kills microbes; a filter captures particulates. Best practice is to use both.

What's the right humidity for my home?

30–50% year-round. Lower in winter (35–40%) to prevent window condensation, higher in summer (45–50%) for comfort and to reduce static.
