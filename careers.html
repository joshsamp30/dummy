Careers at Eric Kjelshus Energy

Join a 50-year-old family-run team of HVAC, plumbing and electrical professionals serving the greater Kansas City area. Competitive pay, ongoing training and real growth opportunities.

  1. Home
  2. Careers

Why Work With Us

Looking to start or advance your career in plumbing, HVAC or electrical? Eric Kjelshus Energy has career opportunities in Lee's Summit, Greenwood and throughout the Kansas City metro. We've been a stable, family-owned business since 1972, and many of our technicians have been with us for 10, 20, even 30+ years. We hire technicians, then we keep them — by paying competitively, investing in training, providing modern tools and trucks, and treating people like adults.

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation — base pay, performance incentives, and overtime that's actually paid.
  • Healthcare benefits for full-time employees.
  • Paid ongoing training — NATE, BPI, manufacturer certifications, IGSHPA, code update classes.
  • Modern, well-maintained service trucks stocked with the parts you actually need.
  • Year-round work — our broad service mix (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, geothermal) keeps every tech busy through every season.
  • Real growth opportunities — many of our senior team started as apprentices.
  • A workplace where integrity matters — we don't expect anyone to up-sell, lie to a customer or sell a repair we don't believe in.

Open Positions

  • HVAC Service Technician — residential and light commercial. NATE preferred. Full-time or part-time.
  • HVAC Installation Technician — furnaces, AC, heat pumps, mini-splits. Experience preferred.
  • Master Plumber — residential, multi-family, light commercial. Missouri license required.
  • Plumbing Apprentice — paid apprenticeship leading to journeyman and master licensing.
  • Master Electrician — Missouri master license required.
  • Geothermal Drill Crew — drilling, loop installation, fusion welding.
  • Customer Service Dispatcher — phone answering, scheduling, customer communication.

What We Look For

  • Honest, ethical character — we run drug tests and background checks on every hire.
  • Mechanical aptitude and a willingness to learn.
  • Good communication skills — our technicians explain problems to customers in plain English.
  • A clean driving record (our techs drive company trucks).
  • Pride in workmanship and a desire to do the work right.

How to Apply

Fill out the form on this page and upload your resume, or call 816-537-5100 to learn more about current openings. A current member of our team will review your information and reach out.

Please email your resume to careers@ericsenergy.com after submitting this form.
