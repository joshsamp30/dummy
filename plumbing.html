Our Plumbing Services

From the smallest leak to a complete repipe, our plumbing services cover every fixture, line and appliance in your home. Below are the most common services we perform every week across the Kansas City metro.

Water Heater Repair & Installation

The average tank-style water heater lasts between 8 and 12 years. When yours starts producing rusty water, knocking, leaking around the base, or running out of hot water faster than it used to, it's time to call us. Our plumbers diagnose the exact cause — failed heating element, sediment buildup, corroded anode rod, faulty thermostat or pressure-relief valve — and either repair the unit or recommend a like-for-like replacement. We install gas, electric and high-efficiency hybrid heat-pump water heaters from every major manufacturer, and we always pull a permit and follow Missouri plumbing code so your installation is safe, warrantied and inspector-ready. Ask about our $50 OFF water heater repair coupon the next time you call.

Tankless Water Heater Repair & Installation

Tankless (on-demand) water heaters heat water only when you need it, eliminating the standby losses that account for up to 30% of a traditional tank's energy use. They take up roughly the space of a carry-on suitcase, deliver an endless supply of hot water, and typically last 20+ years with annual descaling. We size, install, repair and service tankless units from Rinnai, Navien, Rheem and Bosch — and we'll talk you honestly through whether tankless is the right call for your household's hot-water demand, gas line size and venting configuration. Switching to tankless almost always cuts hot-water energy use by 24–34% versus a comparable storage-tank model.

Burst Pipe & Water Leak Repair

A single burst pipe can release more than 12 gallons of water per minute. Whether the cause is a winter freeze, a corroded fitting or shifting foundation pressure, our 24/7 emergency plumbers will shut off your supply, isolate the damage and complete the repair the same day in nearly every case. We carry copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanised replacement materials on every truck, plus thermal imaging and acoustic leak-detection equipment for finding hidden leaks behind drywall or under slabs without unnecessary demolition.

Sump Pump Installation, Repair & Battery Backup

If your basement has flooded once, it can flood again. Our team installs primary sump pumps sized to your basement's footprint and water table, plus battery-backup systems that keep pumping during the power outages that often accompany severe storms. We also offer Wi-Fi-connected pumps that text you the moment a problem is detected. Annual sump pump testing is part of our maintenance offering and dramatically reduces your risk of basement flooding.

Gas Leak Detection & Repair

Natural gas leaks are uncommon, but when they happen they're life-threatening. We use combustible-gas analysers that locate the smallest leak in supply lines, valves, fittings and appliances. If you smell sulphur or rotten eggs in your home, leave immediately and call your gas utility, then call us. Eric Kjelshus is one of the few contractors in the metro qualified to perform combustion analysis on gas-fired heating equipment and chimney systems — a niche that has saved many local families from carbon-monoxide exposure over the years.

Kitchen Plumbing & Garbage Disposal Services

Garbage disposals, dishwasher hookups, ice-maker lines, kitchen faucets, under-sink shutoff valves, P-traps and drain lines — we handle every kitchen plumbing need. The most common kitchen issue we see is a clogged or jammed disposal that's actually fine; the second most common is a slow drain caused by grease and food buildup that requires professional jetting. Either way, we resolve it cleanly and warranty the work.

Sump Pumps & Sewer Line Services

Tree roots, ageing clay or cast-iron sewer lines, and shifting soil are the leading causes of sewer backups in older Kansas City neighbourhoods. We use sewer cameras to inspect the line, hydro-jetting to clear it, and trenchless replacement methods (where appropriate) to avoid digging up your yard. Recurring backups, gurgling drains, sewage smells and patchy lawn growth are all signs that something is wrong below the surface.

Water Filtration & Softening Systems

Kansas City's water is on the harder end of the national spectrum, with calcium and magnesium levels that scale fixtures, shorten the life of water heaters and dishwashers, and leave skin and hair feeling dry. We install whole-home water softeners, reverse-osmosis drinking systems and carbon-block filtration that remove chlorine, chloramines and microplastics. After installation we test the treated water in your kitchen so you can see the difference yourself.

Signs You Need a Plumber Today

Plumbing rarely fails without warning. The earlier you call, the smaller the bill — and the lower the chance of secondary water damage. Schedule a visit if you notice any of the following:

  • Hot water that runs out faster than it used to or never gets truly hot
  • Rust-coloured, cloudy or metallic-tasting water from any tap
  • A persistent drip, hissing sound or moisture spot on a wall or ceiling
  • Drain lines that gurgle, drain slowly or back up into other fixtures
  • An unexplained spike in your water bill with no change in usage
  • Pooling water near the water heater, washer, sump pit or basement floor drain
  • The smell of sulphur (gas), sewage, or musty mildew anywhere in the home
  • A sump pump that runs constantly, runs not at all, or trips its breaker

Frequently Asked Plumbing Questions

How long does a water heater last?

Tank-style water heaters typically last 8–12 years; tankless models last 15–20 years with regular maintenance. Hard water, high household demand and skipping the annual flush all shorten lifespan.

How can I tell if I have a hidden water leak?

Turn off every fixture and water-using appliance, then watch your water meter for 30 minutes. If it moves at all, you have a leak. Our thermal-imaging and acoustic-leak tools can pinpoint the exact location without unnecessary demolition.

How often should I have my plumbing inspected?

We recommend a whole-home plumbing inspection every two years for homes under 20 years old, and annually for older homes. Inspections catch corroded shutoff valves, slow leaks and ageing supply lines before they fail.

Do you offer 24-hour emergency plumbing?

Yes. A live person answers our phone 24/7/365 and dispatches a technician immediately for true emergencies — burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer backups and total loss of hot water in cold weather.
