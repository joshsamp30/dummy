FAQ

Answers from our master technicians.

Don't see your question? Send it over — we usually reply within an hour.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency HVAC service?

Yes. A live technician answers around the clock, every day of the year — including holidays. Call 816-537-5100.

How much can I save with a geothermal system?

Most customers see up to 90% savings on heating and cooling costs versus conventional systems. The federal tax credit covers 30% of installation through 2032.

What areas do you serve?

The greater Kansas City metro: Greenwood, Lee's Summit, Blue Springs, Independence, Raytown, Grain Valley, Belton, Raymore and surrounding communities.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — flexible terms on qualifying installations including geothermal, furnaces, AC and water heaters, with approved credit. Ask about 0% promotions.

Are your technicians licensed?

Every tech is licensed, bonded and insured. Our geothermal designers are IGSHPA-accredited and our electricians carry Missouri master licenses.
