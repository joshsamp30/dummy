About us

Saving energy one building at a time — since 1972.

Founded by Eric Kjelshus in 1972, our family-run team has spent five decades engineering high-efficiency comfort systems for homes and businesses across the Kansas City metro. We're best known for award-winning geothermal installations that slash energy costs by up to 90% — but we treat every furnace tune-up and water-heater swap with the same care.

Eric himself holds 50+ certificates of achievement and is the only HVAC scientist in the region with this distinction. He has been featured on NBC and ABC for his work on building performance and helped shape national building standards.
