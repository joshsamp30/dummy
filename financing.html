Flexible Financing for HVAC & Home Comfort

Get pre-approved through our Optimus contractor financing partner with a soft credit pull that doesn't appear as an inquiry on your credit report.

  1. Home
  2. Financing

A new HVAC system, geothermal install or whole-home water heater upgrade is a big purchase, and most families don't want to drain their savings to pay for it all at once. Eric Kjelshus Energy partners with Optimus Contractor Financing to make HVAC, plumbing, electrical and geothermal projects affordable through flexible monthly payments. The application process takes a few minutes online, uses a soft credit pull (which does not appear as an inquiry on your credit report), and gives you pre-approval before you ever meet with a technician.

How Optimus Financing Works

  1. Apply online at the Optimus portal — soft credit check, takes about 5 minutes.
  2. Get pre-approved usually within minutes. Pre-approval is good for a set time window so you can shop and plan.
  3. Meet with our technician for an estimate or service call. Discuss financing options, terms, monthly payment and any applicable promotions.
  4. Schedule the work once you've selected the right financing option.
  5. Pay your monthly bill directly to Optimus via their portal.

What's Eligible for Financing?

  • Air conditioning installation and replacement
  • Heating installation, including furnaces, boilers and heat pumps
  • Geothermal heating and cooling systems
  • Water heater replacement (tank-style and tankless)
  • Whole-home generators
  • Indoor air quality systems including whole-home filtration, humidifiers and dehumidifiers
  • Ductwork redesign and major repairs
  • Electrical panel upgrades
  • EV charger installations
  • Larger plumbing repairs and repipes

Stack Financing With Tax Credits and Rebates

Many of our customers combine financing with federal tax credits and utility rebates to dramatically reduce the effective cost of their projects. Common stacks include:

  • Geothermal: 30% federal tax credit through 2032 + Evergy ground-source heat pump rebate + financed monthly payment.
  • Insulation & air sealing: Up to $1,200/year federal 25C tax credit + financing for the upfront cost.
  • High-efficiency AC: $550–$800 Evergy rebate + 30% tax credit (qualifying systems) + financing.
  • Heat pumps: Up to $2,000 federal tax credit + utility rebate + financing.

Financing FAQ

Will applying hurt my credit?

No. Optimus uses a soft credit inquiry that doesn't appear on your credit report or affect your score.

What credit score do I need?

Optimus offers products across the credit spectrum. The best terms (lowest rates, longest 0% promotions) generally require strong credit, but many customers with average credit qualify for very competitive financing.

Are there application fees?

No application fees. Closing costs and origination fees vary by product and are disclosed up front before you sign.

Can I pay off the loan early?

Yes. Most Optimus products allow early payoff without prepayment penalty. Check the specific product documentation when you apply.
