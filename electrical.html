Electrical problems are uniquely dangerous because the symptoms — a buzzing outlet, a warm switch plate, an occasional breaker trip — often look minor right up until a fire starts. Modern homes also place loads on electrical systems that the original builder never anticipated: induction ranges, EV chargers, heat pumps, hot tubs, multi-zone HVAC, home offices full of computer equipment. Our recommendation is simple: if anything feels off, call us. We'll diagnose, explain it in plain English and quote the fix before any work starts.

LED Lighting Installation & Retrofit

LED lighting is the single highest-ROI electrical upgrade you can make. Compared to incandescent bulbs, LEDs use 75–90% less electricity, last 25 times longer, and produce far less heat (which means less load on your air conditioner in summer). We install and retrofit LED lighting throughout the home — recessed cans, under-cabinet, pendants, exterior security lighting, landscape lighting and full-house relamping. Existing fixtures can usually be retrofit with LED replacement bulbs or panels rather than completely replaced, saving labour and material cost.

Whole-Home Generator Installation

Power outages in the Kansas City metro happen — wind, ice, summer storms, equipment failures upstream. A standby generator (usually fueled by natural gas or propane) wired into your electrical system through an automatic transfer switch will start within seconds of an outage and run essential or whole-home loads for as long as the storm lasts. We install Generac, Kohler and Briggs & Stratton generators sized to your specific home, plus the gas line work, concrete pad, transfer switch and code-compliant wiring. Generator installations also qualify for Missouri property tax exemptions in many counties.

Surge Protection

Whole-home surge protection installed at your electrical panel stops voltage spikes (from lightning, utility issues or large appliance startups) before they reach your sensitive electronics. The cost is modest — typically a few hundred dollars installed — and the protection covers everything plugged into your home: HVAC, refrigerator, computers, TVs, smart home gear, EV chargers. Plug-in surge strips are a useful second line of defence but they don't replace whole-home protection.

EV Charger Installation

If you've bought (or are about to buy) an electric vehicle, a Level 2 home charger is the single best upgrade you can make. Charging speeds go from "all night" on a 120V outlet to "fully charged in 4–6 hours" on a properly installed 240V Level 2 circuit. We install EV chargers from Tesla, ChargePoint, JuiceBox and Wallbox and we'll spec the right amperage for your vehicle and your panel's available capacity.

Electrical Panel Upgrades

Older Kansas City homes often have 100-amp service that's adequate for a 1970s lifestyle but overloaded today. We perform full panel upgrades to 200-amp or 400-amp service, replace obsolete or recalled panels (Federal Pacific, Zinsco, certain older Pushmatic models), add sub-panels for additions and detached garages, and install new circuits for HVAC, EV charging, hot tubs, kitchens and home offices.

Electrical Repair & Troubleshooting

The most common electrical service calls we get are flickering lights (often loose neutrals or dimmer/LED incompatibility), recurring breaker trips (overloaded circuits, ground faults or failing breakers), warm switch plates (dangerous loose connection, fix immediately), GFCI outlets that won't reset (could be a tripped GFCI upstream or a real ground fault), and "half the house lost power" symptoms (commonly a bad neutral connection at the panel or meter base, which is a serious issue requiring same-day attention).

Carbon Monoxide & Smoke Detector Installation

Modern building codes require interconnected, hardwired smoke and CO detectors with battery backup in specific locations throughout the home. We install code-compliant detector networks during remodels and as upgrades to older homes that still rely on standalone battery units.

Electrical FAQ

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?

Frequent breaker trips, lights that dim when major appliances start, no available breaker spaces left in the panel, the panel itself is a recalled brand (Federal Pacific, Zinsco, certain Pushmatic), or you're adding major loads (EV charger, heat pump, induction range, hot tub).

Are flickering lights a big deal?

Sometimes minor (LED/dimmer incompatibility), sometimes serious (loose neutral connection in the panel — a fire risk). Have it diagnosed.

How long does a generator installation take?

Typically 1–2 days on-site for the equipment, plus permitting and gas-line coordination. From signed contract to commissioned generator is usually 4–8 weeks depending on equipment availability.

Do I need a permit for electrical work?

For anything beyond like-for-like replacement of fixtures and devices, yes. We pull permits as required by the jurisdiction and meet inspectors on-site.
