Lee's Summit has grown from a quiet rail town to one of the largest and most desirable suburbs in the Kansas City metro. We've been serving Lee's Summit since 1972, dispatching from our nearby Greenwood headquarters, and we know the typical issues unique to each neighbourhood.

Cooling Services in Lee's Summit

Air conditioner repair, central AC installation, ductless mini-split installation and AC tune-ups for Lee's Summit homes. Common Lee's Summit calls include refrigerant leaks on aging systems, condensate pan overflows triggering float switches, oversized AC units that short-cycle and fail to dehumidify, and ductwork-pressure issues in larger two-story homes. Our NATE-certified technicians arrive in stocked trucks with the parts needed for most repairs in a single visit. View AC services.

Heating Services in Lee's Summit

Furnace repair, heating maintenance, heat pump installation, boiler service and radiant floor heat. With the wide age range of Lee's Summit homes, we see everything from 1980s 80% AFUE furnaces ready for replacement, to mid-2000s 90% AFUE units needing routine service, to brand-new 98% modulating furnaces that just need filter changes and annual inspections. Plumbing in Lee's Summit

Water heater replacement (tank and tankless), burst pipe repair, sump pumps, gas leak detection, kitchen and bathroom plumbing, and water filtration. Newer subdivisions usually have PEX supply and PVC waste — easier to service but with their own quirks. View plumbing services.

Indoor Air Quality & Energy

Lee's Summit's mix of older drafty homes and newer tight homes both have IAQ challenges. Older homes often have CO concerns from atmospheric-vented appliances; newer homes often need active humidity control and ventilation upgrades. We perform comprehensive IAQ assessments, install whole-home filtration, humidifiers, dehumidifiers and UV systems. We also offer comprehensive BPI-certified energy audits with $100 OFF coupon, including blower door testing and duct leakage testing. View IAQ services.

Geothermal Installations in Lee's Summit

Many Lee's Summit lots are large enough to accommodate horizontal geothermal loops, and we've installed numerous systems across Chapman Farms, Lakewood and surrounding subdivisions. With the 30% federal tax credit and our flexible financing, geothermal often costs less per month than the conventional system it replaces — while delivering up to 90% energy savings. View geothermal services.
