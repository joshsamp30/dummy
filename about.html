50+ Years Serving Kansas City

Eric Kjelshus founded Eric Kjelshus Energy Heating and Cooling in 1972 with a simple idea: do the work right the first time, treat customers honestly, and never up-sell something they don't need. More than five decades later, that same philosophy guides our 30+ technicians, designers, drillers, plumbers and electricians as we serve homes and businesses across the Kansas City metro. We've grown from a single truck to a full-service mechanical contractor, but we still answer the phone with a real human, every hour of every day.

Our work spans residential and commercial heating, air conditioning, plumbing, electrical, geothermal, solar, indoor air quality, energy audits, weatherization, insulation and renewable-energy systems. We install Energy Star–qualified equipment, design for efficiency from the start, and handle every job ourselves — no subcontractors who don't share our standards.

About Eric Kjelshus

Is there a science to HVAC? Yes. Eric Kjelshus is a perfect example of an HVAC Scientist — what the industry more commonly calls an HVAC Engineer. He designs, installs and maintains heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems by applying the scientific principles of thermodynamics, fluid mechanics and heat transfer to optimise comfort, indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

Since 1972, Eric has been pursuing knowledge in this field. He currently holds over 50 certificates of achievement in HVAC, building science and energy efficiency — the only scientist in the Kansas City region with this distinction. He's a master mechanical contractor and master pipe fitter with multiple certifications from:

  • NATE — North American Technician Excellence
  • RSES — Refrigeration Service Engineers Society
  • BPI — Building Performance Institute
  • NCI — National Comfort Institute
  • IGSHPA — International Ground Source Heat Pump Association

Eric's analytical approach lets him run multiple diagnostics on every project, evaluating each scenario individually to determine the best outcome for efficiency and comfort in the ever-changing weather of the Midwest. He has shared his knowledge with up-and-coming technicians by teaching at several Kansas City community colleges and at the national level. He has been interviewed by NBC and ABC about building performance, and he has been instrumental in changing national building standards.
