Greenwood-Specific HVAC & Plumbing Considerations

Greenwood's housing stock is a mix of mid-century homes with original or replacement HVAC, post-1980s subdivisions with mature systems now reaching end-of-life, and newer construction with high-efficiency equipment. Our experience across all three eras means we can quickly recognise the typical issues each presents:

  • Older homes often have undersized ductwork and panels that struggle to keep up with modern AC and electric demand. Panel upgrades and duct redesign are common.
  • Established subdivisions are at the age where original furnaces and ACs are reaching 20+ years and replacement is overdue. Geothermal upgrades make excellent sense given lot sizes and clay-soil conditions.
  • Newer homes are typically tight, well-insulated and benefit from added IAQ equipment — humidity control, HRV/ERV, and balanced ventilation.

Geothermal in Greenwood

Greenwood lots are typically generous enough to accommodate horizontal geothermal loops, and the local clay soil has good thermal conductivity for ground-source heat exchange. We've installed dozens of geothermal systems throughout Greenwood and have seen customers cut their heating and cooling bills by 60–90% versus conventional equipment. With the 30% federal tax credit available through 2032, now is the most financially attractive time to install geothermal we've seen in two decades.
