Commercial HVAC, Plumbing & Refrigeration

One trusted contractor for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, refrigeration, walk-in coolers and water heaters across restaurants, offices, retail, multi-family and light industrial buildings.

For commercial property owners and facilities managers, downtime is revenue lost. A walk-in cooler that fails on a Friday night, a rooftop unit that quits on a 95-degree afternoon, a backed-up sewer line in a busy restaurant — every one of these is an emergency that costs you money the moment it starts. Eric Kjelshus Energy has spent more than 50 years keeping Kansas City businesses running. We answer the phone live 24/7, dispatch promptly, and bring trucks stocked for the most common commercial repairs. We also offer planned-maintenance contracts that catch most failures before they become emergencies.

We work with property managers, restaurant owners, retailers, offices, multi-family operators and light industrial customers across the Kansas City metro. Our team is licensed, bonded and insured to commercial standards, and we coordinate with your facilities team, tenants and code officials to minimise disruption.

Rooftop HVAC service

Commercial HVAC

Rooftop units, split systems, VRF/VRV systems, mini-splits, dedicated outdoor air systems (DOAS), make-up air, exhaust fans and economizer controls. We service every major brand including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Daikin, York and Mitsubishi. Whether you need same-day repair, scheduled maintenance, a new install or a complete system replacement, our commercial HVAC technicians have the equipment and experience to handle it. Most repairs are completed on the first visit because our service trucks carry capacitors, contactors, motors, controls and refrigerant for the equipment we see in the field every day.

Commercial Plumbing

Commercial water heaters (gas, electric, tankless), boilers, sump pumps, lift stations, sewer lines, hydro-jetting, backflow prevention, fixture installation and emergency leak repair. We're equipped to handle multi-fixture commercial plumbing where coordinated shutdown and rapid restoration of service are critical — restaurants, multi-family, offices and retail. Our commercial plumbers work nights and weekends when needed to avoid disrupting your operations.

Commercial Refrigeration

Walk-in coolers and freezers, reach-in refrigerators, ice machines, refrigerated cases, prep tables and beverage equipment. Refrigeration is mission-critical for restaurants, grocery stores, convenience stores and any business that holds perishable inventory. We diagnose and repair compressor, condenser, evaporator, refrigerant, electrical and control issues — and we'll talk honestly about repair-vs-replace economics on aging equipment.

Light Commercial Electrical

Commercial panel upgrades, sub-panel installation, branch circuits, EV charger installation, exterior and parking-lot lighting, LED retrofits and code-compliance upgrades. We pull permits, meet inspectors and coordinate with your other trades to keep your project on schedule.

Indoor Air Quality for Commercial Buildings

Post-pandemic, commercial IAQ is a strategic priority for many tenants. We install MERV-13 and HEPA filtration upgrades, UV-C systems on coil assemblies, demand-controlled ventilation tied to CO2 sensors, and dedicated outdoor air systems for buildings where ventilation rates are below ASHRAE 62.1 standards.

Planned Maintenance Contracts

Our commercial maintenance contracts are tailored to your equipment list, hours of operation and budget. Typical contracts include quarterly preventive visits on rooftops and split systems, twice-annual coil cleaning, monthly filter changes, semi-annual refrigerant-pressure checks on refrigeration equipment, and priority dispatch with no overtime charges for contract holders. Customers on contract typically see 20–35% lower annual repair costs versus break-fix, and equipment lasts 30–50% longer.
